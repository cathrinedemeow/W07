# Global Event Ticket Reservation System

## 1. Product Vision
### Problem
Many older adults find modern online ticket systems difficult to use. Small text, complicated menus, registration forms, and too many steps can make it difficult for them to buy tickets. Because of this, some older adults may attend fewer concerts, theatre performances, exhibitions, and other events.
### Vision
**FOR** older adults  
**WHO** find online ticket systems difficult to use  
**THE** Global Event Ticket Reservation System  
**IS A** simple and accessible ticket reservation platform  
**THAT** helps older adults find events, choose comfortable seats, and reserve tickets easily  
**UNLIKE** many existing ticket platforms  
**OUR PRODUCT** provides a simple interface and recommends suitable and affordable events.

## 2. Users and Stakeholders

**USERES** - Older Adults, Family Members
**STAKEHOLDERS**
Attendee - Find events, choose a suitable seat, and reserve a ticker easly.
Helper - Help an older relative when needed.
Event Organizer - Create events and manage ticket sales.
Venue Manager - Help users with problems and cancrllation.
Administraror - Manage the platform.
Payment Provider - Process payment securely
## 3. Personas

### Persona 1 - Helen, 67 years old
**Occupation:** Retired music teacher.
**Technology:** Uses a smartphone, but has limited experience with online services.
**Background:** Helen really enjoys concerts and theatre, but she often asks her son to buy ticket, because booking is difficult for her.
**Goal:** She wants to find events and buy tickets by herself.
**Difficulties:** 
		1. Small text and buttons
		2. Compicated menus
		3. Too many steps
		4. Unclear seat information
### Persona 2 - Sara, 39 years old
**Occupation:** Accountant.
**Technology:** Comfortable with computers and tecnologies.
**Background:** Sara often help her mother with online services.
**Goal:** She wants to help her mom book tickets without doing everything for her.
**Difficulties:** 
		1. Difficult to explain complicated websites over the phone.
		2. No simple way to help another person book a ticket.
### Persona 3 - Rudi, 44 years old
**Occupation:** Event Organizer.
**Technology:** Uses computers everyday.
**Background:** Rudi organized concerts and cultural events.
**Goal:** He wants more people, including older adults, to discover and attend his events.
**Difficulties:** 
		1. Some customers may give up during the booking process
		2. Smaller or cheaper events may be difficult for customers to discover
## 4. Scenarios

### Scenario 1 - Finding Event
Helen wants to go to a theatre performance but does not know what events are available. She opens the system and sees a simple list of events with clear information about the date, time, location, and price. She finds a performance she likes and opens its page.
### Scenario 2 - Cheaper Event
Helen likes a concert, but the ticket is too expensive. The system recommends similar events with lower prices. Elena finds another concert that she likes and decides to book it.
